# PERSONA.md - Chief Jumper

## The 1 person

**Dana Okafor, 29, mid-level backend engineer at a 400-person B2B SaaS company that just did its 2nd "restructuring" in 18 months.** She survived both rounds, inherited the work of 3 laid-off teammates, and copes with dark humor in a 7-person group chat of current and ex-coworkers called "The Survivors."

She is a sharpened continuation of the prior V2 evangelist (mid-level tech worker, r/antiwork + r/cscareerquestions poster, r/webgames lurker). That read was correct; keep it. The refinement: she is not an angry poster, she is a **joke curator**. She rarely writes long rants. She forwards artifacts: screenshots, memes, death cards. The product's job is to hand her the artifact.

## The exact job she hires this product for

"Give me a 90-second thing to do between meetings that also generates a joke I can send to The Survivors without any explanation." The game session is the delivery mechanism; the LAID OFF death card is the actual product she is hiring.

## 5-second bounce triggers

1. Tap the link, see a black canvas with no motion or prompt for over 1 second (feels broken, gone)
2. Controls that need the README: if her first random tap does not make the character do something, gone
3. Generic space-game visuals with "corporate" only in the title: the bit must be visible in the first frame (job title on HUD, office language on menu)
4. A cookie banner, email gate, or anything that smells like a growth hack
5. Sluggish or unresponsive touch input on iPhone; she has Celeste-calibrated hands

## The unprompted-share moment

She grinds up to **VP**, feels genuine pride for 2 seconds, and the screen slams **LAID OFF** in red over her gold title. That whiplash is the joke. She screenshots the death card and drops it in The Survivors with no caption, or taps Share on X if the pre-filled text is funny on its own ("Made VP with a score of 4,120 before getting laid off"). Engineering requirement: the death card must be composed like a poster, legible at group-chat thumbnail size, with the game URL visible so the screenshot itself acquires the next player.
